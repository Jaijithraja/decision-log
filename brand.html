Brand Identity & Design System

An organizational memory product for teams. Capturing decisions, preserving the reasoning behind them, and reconnecting context to future work.

1. Symbol Progression & Conceptual Rationale

The mark avoids all cliché tech tropes (no brains, books, chat bubbles, or AI stars). Instead, it translates the three core stages of organizational memory into three translucent, overlapping geometric bodies that coalesce into an intuitive, understated "L" gesture.

1
Conversation

Transient dialogue occurring across Slack, meetings, and tickets.

2
Understanding

Distilling core rationale, rejected alternatives, and consensus context.

3
Preserved Knowledge

The enduring institutional memory locked into the foundation — forming the subtle "L".
